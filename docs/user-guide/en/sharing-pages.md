# Sharing pages

You can let someone view (or edit) **one page and its subpages** without adding them to the whole space — for example to give an external partner a set of contract documents.

## Who can share

- Only **space admins** (and system admins) see the **Share** button on a page.
- People you share with get one of two access levels: **Can view** or **Can edit**. A share never grants admin rights.
- A person's actual access to a page is the **highest** of their space access and their shares. Sharing only **adds** access; it never lowers what someone already has.

## Share a page

1. Open the page and click **Share** at the top right.
2. Enter an email, pick **Can view** or **Can edit**, then click **Share**.
   - An email that **already has a KB account** gets access at once and receives a notification email with a link to the page.
   - An email **without an account** receives an **invitation email** (valid 14 days). The invitation link is also shown in the dialog so you can send it yourself if the email does not arrive. The recipient signs in with Google using that exact email, clicks **Accept invitation** and lands on the page.
3. The dialog lists **People with access** (change access or stop sharing) and **Pending invitations** (resend or revoke).

People who have access through a parent page show **"Shared through the parent page …"**; to change it, open that parent page.

## What people you share with see

- **Shared with me** in the sidebar lists the pages shared directly with them; use the arrow to see subpages.
- They can open, search, read the version history and the attachments of the shared page and its subpages.
- They see the **name** of the space that holds the page but **cannot** open the space, other pages, its member list or its trash.
- With **Can edit** they edit the content, title and icon; they cannot move, delete or create pages.

## Notes

- When a shared page goes to the **trash**, people it is shared with lose access until it is restored. Deleting the page for good removes its shares.
- A **locked** account can no longer use its shares; pages cannot be shared with locked or rejected accounts.
- Every share, access change and removal is recorded in the space **activity log**.
