# Navigation and the home page

## Sidebar

The dark sidebar is on the left of the screen (on a phone, tap the menu icon at the top left to open it).

- **Home**, **Search** and **What's new** at the top; the one you are on is highlighted.
- **Spaces**: the Spaces you can view, each with its own icon. Click a Space to open its page tree right below it.
- **Create space** (people inside the organization only) at the end of the list.
- At the bottom is the **running version**; click it to see **What's new**.

## Top bar

- **Search field** (shortcut `Ctrl + K`, `⌘ + K` on a Mac): opens the quick switcher. Type a title or some content — accents are optional; use the arrow keys to choose and `Enter` to open. With an empty box it lists the pages you opened recently.
- **VI / EN**: switches the interface language (a language icon on a phone). The choice is remembered for your account.
- **Light / dark**: switches the light or dark theme.
- **Avatar**: Settings, What's new, Administration (admins only) and Sign out.

## Home page

The home page has three parts:

1. **Large search box**: click it to open the quick switcher, the same as the field in the top bar.
2. **Spaces**: each card shows the icon, name, description, who can view it and your role. People inside the organization get a **Create space** button at the top of this part.
3. **Recently updated**: up to 8 pages edited most recently in the Spaces you can view, with their Space and when they were edited. Click a row to open the page. This part is hidden while you have no Space.

You only see the Spaces and pages you are allowed to view; pages in the trash do not appear.
