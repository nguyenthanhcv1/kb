# Space access

Who can view or edit a Space is decided by its **Space admins** in the Space's **Settings › Members**. There are three ways to give access:

1. **Members** — add individual internal users (search by name or email, accents optional) as _Can view_, _Can edit_ or _Full access_.
2. **Groups with access** — give a whole group (for example “KAS”) the _Can view_ or _Can edit_ role.
3. **Invite guests by email** — people outside the organization get an invite link and can only get _Can view_ or _Can edit_.

## Give a group access

1. Open the Space › **Settings** › **Members** and scroll to **Groups with access**.
2. Pick the **Group role** (_Can view_ or _Can edit_), then type the group's name in **Search groups**.
3. Click **Add** next to the group.

Every member of the group gets that access right away, including people who join the group later. People who leave the group lose the access it gave them. Change a group's role with the role picker on its row; click **Remove access** to take it back.

Good to know:

- A person can have their own role and roles through one or more groups — they get the **highest** of them. Removing a group's access does not affect anyone's own role or access through other groups.
- A group never gets _Full access_ (Space admin); admins are always named people.
- Guests and accounts waiting for approval cannot be in a group.
- Granting, changing and removing a group's access is recorded in the Space's **Activity log**.

## Managing groups (super admins)

Super admins manage groups and their members in **Administration › Groups**:

- The group list shows each group's number of members and of Spaces it has access to.
- A group's page has a **Spaces with access** section: the Spaces the group can reach and its role there. Click a Space to open its members settings.
- When you **Delete group**, the confirmation lists how many members and which Spaces lose the access the group gave.
